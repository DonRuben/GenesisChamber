Configuration Philosophy
Speed vs. Depth

🎯 Core Principle

Creative souls should be FAST. Ogilvy, Rand, Musk — they think from instinct and experience. Deep thinking adds 30-90s per soul without improving creative output. Save thinking for roles that analyze, critique, or synthesize: DA, Moderator, Evaluator.

⏱️ Speed Impact

Thinking OFF ~8-15s per soul
Thinking ON ~20-40s per soul
Deep Thinking ~45-90s per soul

With 5 souls × Deep = 4-7 min for Stage 1 alone
